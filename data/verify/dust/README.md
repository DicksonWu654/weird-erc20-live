# Liquidity-floor and protocol-fee aggregates

The tables describe the superseded legacy selected-pool/+7200 design, floor/venue sensitivities and integer protocol-fee formula checks. They do not replace launch-state estimates. `fee_activation.json` pins the observed activation; its evidence paths identify retained local source evidence, not public files. `fee_mint_formula.csv` retains numeric formula inputs/results for ten independent checks, removing sample IDs and transaction/evidence pointers. `venue_by_year.csv` keeps descriptive below-tiny-floor and liquidity-decline screen shares; neither implies attributed LP removal. Other CSVs provide grouped monthly, floor, denominator and validation summaries. Per-token selections, reserve samples and provider/RPC bodies are omitted.
