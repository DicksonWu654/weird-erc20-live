# Independent launch-state replay: publication aggregates

The independent harness samples 30 states per available year × venue × predicted-harm/not-harm cell, or all when fewer exist. Predictions are sealed until verdicts freeze. The selected state is first positive liquidity +300; the fixed 0.01 ETH buy, approval and sell share the following synthetic block. This differs from the production sell ladder. V4 real quote principal is reconstructed from position events independently.

`summary.csv` preserves raw and weighted confusion totals, sample-conditional precision/NPV, weighted sensitivity and support, and explicit no-liquidity, failed-buy and unknown counts. Real-floor, virtual-floor, all-sample and nominal-rounding domains are distinct. Wilson/Kish and Bonferroni component-envelope intervals are conditional approximations, not population recall guarantees. They omit census uncertainty, launch dependence and classification error. Fewer than 30 effective trials are withheld in the paper/site.

`sample_counts.csv` contains the selected-state count. `comparison_groups.csv` groups year, venue, real/virtual-floor predicates, independent verdict, rounding-adjusted verdict and sealed prediction, retaining only count and summed analysis weight. The public checker independently reconstructs all saved confusion cells and weighted point metrics from these groups. No token, pool, sample identifier, per-token prediction or raw request is published.

The analysis weight is the original census expansion weight times prediction-cell population divided by replay sample size. The private verdict-freeze hash is validated before grouping. `report_manifest.json` records the original freeze/selection digests and the public comparison-group digest; `data/release-projections.json` records the original comparison digest. `audit.json` is the saved upstream audit summary. These records do not provide the omitted raw observations or permit rerunning private selection/freeze checks from public aggregates.
